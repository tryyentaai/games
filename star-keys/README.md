# Star Keys

A piano-meets-rhythm game for kids 5-9 (built for 7). Glowing star notes fall onto a real piano layout. Every note has one color forever, so kids learn C D E F G A B C by color, letter, position and sound.

## How it plays
- **Go!** always picks the next good step.
- Each of 9 songs has three stars:
  - **Learn** (turtle): notes stop on the line and wait for the right key. No timer.
  - **Play** (comet): notes flow with a beat, Guitar Hero style. After two misses the next note waits, so a kid never misses three in a row. Tempo and timing window adjust quietly.
  - **Echo** (bubbles): Pip plays a short phrase, the kid plays it back from memory. Phrases grow as the kid gets better.
- A mini music staff shows upcoming notes, linking sheet music to keys.
- Hold long notes for a bonus. Streaks trigger Super Star mode. Golden notes are rare treats.
- Stars unlock sounds (Music Box, Bells, Kitty, Space) and worlds (Candy, Ocean, Forest, Gold). Stickers are earned per song; tap one to hear the song.
- Stuck? Pulses at 5 s, a spoken nudge at 10 s, a pointing hand after 3 wrong tries. The ? button jumps ahead.

Songs (public domain): Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells, When the Saints, Twinkle Twinkle, London Bridge, Old MacDonald, Row Your Boat.

## Install on iPad or iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Share → Add to Home Screen.
3. Launch it once while online. After that it works offline.

## Grown-ups panel
Hold the gear on the home screen for 2 seconds: kid's name (used by the voice), sound / beat / voice, help level, key labels (C D E, Do Re Mi, or none), a last-song nudge after 3/5/8 songs, and a real piano connection.

## Real piano
A USB/Bluetooth MIDI keyboard works in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Connect MIDI in the grown-ups panel). iPad Safari does not support Web MIDI. A computer keyboard also works: A S D F G H J K = C to high C.

## Tip
Turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) to keep your kid inside the game. Check the iPad silent switch if there is no sound.

No ads, purchases, links, accounts or data collection. Progress saves on the device.
