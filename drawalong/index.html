About drawings in this app

Delete removes this drawing — its lines, colouring, and picture — from this app on this device, and clears it from the current session. Copies you already shared or saved outside the app will not be deleted.

Draw With Me is made for this household. Drawings live only on this device. Sharing the app's link with other families, or adding accounts, sync, or sharing features for children, reopens the privacy review behind it.

Drawings on this phone

Drawings are saved only on this phone, in this browser. Safari can delete them if storage runs low or the app isn't used for a while, and clearing browser data removes them. To keep a drawing for good, share its PNG from the Grown-ups area.
