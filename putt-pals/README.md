# Putt Pals

Mini golf for kids 5–9. Your golfer and an animal pal play 4-hole rounds across Meadow, Beach and Snow courses.

## How it plays
- **Pull back, let go.** Drag anywhere on the screen. The dotted line shows where the ball will roll, and the ring shows how hard you're hitting.
- Each world adds one new idea: bumpers, bank shots and a spinning log in the Meadow; sand, water and an island green at the Beach; ice, a portal and a snowball maze in the Snow.
- Water only splashes the ball back to where it was. There are no lives, no timers and no game over.
- A hidden helper keeps a kid from missing 3 times in a row. Difficulty adjusts quietly as they play.
- **Pal power:** two holes-in-one in a row (or a rare surprise) gives the next shot a rainbow trail that goes in.
- **Help:** the ? button shows which way to pull. Idle kids get a pulse, then a spoken nudge and a hand demo. After 2 misses, a golden line shows a good shot.
- **Undo** (bottom-left) puts the ball back where it was.
- **Collect:** every round unlocks a new pal (Ziggy, Mo, Fizz, Hops, Ty, Koko). Gold stars on the course unlock new ball colors. Progress is saved on the device and never resets.
- After the first 12 holes, the courses come back flipped.

## Install on iPhone or iPad
1. Open the game in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it once while online. After that it works offline.

## Grown-ups panel
Hold the gear on the home screen for 2 seconds. You can set:
- sound, music and voice
- help level (More, Normal, Less)
- your kid's name, which the voice uses
- a break reminder after 1–3 rounds
- a different golfer, or start over

## Good to know
- No ads, purchases, links, accounts or data collection.
- If there's no sound on iPhone, check the silent switch.
- **Guided Access** (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) keeps your kid inside the game.
