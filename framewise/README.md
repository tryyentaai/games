# Framewise (web)

A camera for people who aren't photographers. Pick a goal, not settings.

**Use it:** open https://tryyentaai.github.io/games/framewise/ in Safari on iPhone, tap Share › Add to Home Screen, and allow camera access.

- **Goals:** Auto, People (2×), Action (keeps the sharpest frame of a 5-shot burst), Scenery (clarity + vibrance), Low Light (aligns and blends 8 frames), Close-up (ultra wide, 2× crop)
- **Live coach:** subject in shadow, white-out highlights, too dark, crooked horizon, hazy or smudged lens, crushed shadows. One-tap fixes where possible
- **Tap to meter:** brightness is set for whatever you tap
- Level, rule-of-thirds grid, mini histogram, pinch zoom, flashlight (where supported), shot report, Learn sheet
- Saves through the share sheet ("Save Image")

The full native iOS app (real aperture control on iPhone 18 Pro, shutter/ISO, 48 MP/RAW, Camera Control) lives in the private `builds-apps` repo under `framewise/`.
