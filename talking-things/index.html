Add a photo

A grown-up can add a photo from the Photos app. The copy stored here is re-made inside the app, which strips location data (EXIF) from it. The original in Photos is untouched — that copy lives outside this app.

Delete this thing?

This removes its photo and its voice from TalkingThings on this phone. It does not delete copies in the Photos app or its cloud backup, photos you have already shared, downloads, or device backups.
