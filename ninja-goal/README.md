# Ninja Goal

A penalty-kick soccer game with ninjas, built for a 5-year-old (no reading needed) with ideas that unlock for older kids.

## How it plays
- **Tap anywhere in the goal** to kick there (swiping up from the ball works too). The ninja runs up, kicks, and an animal-ninja goalie dives.
- A match is **8 kicks**, then a party: trophy, stars, belt progress and any new unlocks. Pick **Again** or **Home**. Nothing starts by itself.
- **No-fail:** no lives, no timers, no misses wide. A save is just "So close!" and the next kick is ready in about 1.5 seconds. A hidden helper keeps success around 75-90% and never allows 3 saves in a row.

### Dojos (one new idea each)
| Dojo | Goalie | The trick | Opens at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bamboo | Bao the panda | He stands to one side. Kick where he is not. | Start |
| Blossom | Kit the fox | She walks side to side. Kick where she is going not to be. | Yellow belt (15 stars) |
| Moon | Hoot the owl | He teleports to a glowing shadow. Kick away from the shadow. | Green belt (60 stars) |
| Snow Peak | Taro the tiger | Too good to beat straight. Kick the gong on the roof first (it's a free kick), he gets dizzy, then score. | Blue belt (90 stars) |

### Rewards
- 1 star per goal. Streaks build: GOAL! → NICE! → SUPER! → AMAZING! → LEGENDARY!
- **Ninja Power:** 3 goals fill the shurikens; the next kick is a slow-motion MEGA GOAL.
- **Golden lanterns** sometimes hang in a top corner. Score through one for bonus stars and a golden scroll.
- **Gold ball** (rare): goal worth 3×.
- **Belts** (White → Gold) unlock headbands, balls and dojos in **My Ninja**. Golden scrolls unlock the Rainbow headband (3) and Moon ball (6). Progress is saved on the device and never resets.

### Help
First game: a hand shows where to tap. If he waits 5 s, sparkly stars mark a good spot; at 10 s the voice gives a hint and the hand comes back. The **?** button does the same on demand.

## Install (iPhone / iPad)
1. Open the game link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it once while online. After that it works offline.

## Grown-ups panel
**Press and hold the gear (top right of the title screen) for 2 seconds.**
- Sound effects, music, voice on/off
- Help level: More / Normal / Less (how often the hidden helper lets a kick slip past)
- Kid's name — the announcer cheers it ("Goal, Leo!")
- Last-round reminder: after 10/15/20 minutes, the next match is the last and ends with "All done!"
- Start over (hold 3 s) — keeps your settings

No ads, purchases, links, accounts or data collection. Everything (art, sound, music) is generated in code.

## Tips
- **iPhone silent switch** can mute game sounds; flip it if there is no sound.
- **Guided Access** keeps him in the game: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On, then triple-click the side button inside the game.
