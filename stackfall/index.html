    HOW TO PLAY

    Fit the falling pieces together. Fill a row edge to edge and it clears. Clear four at once for a QUAD. The stack reaches the top, game over. Every 10 lines the level goes up and pieces fall faster.

    TOUCH

    Flick downHard drop
    Flick upHold
