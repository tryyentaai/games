# Hoop Pop

A carnival hoop-toss game for kids 5–9. Pop-a-shot meets ski-ball: tap or swipe to throw, and the higher and farther the hoop, the more it's worth.

## How it plays

- **Throw:** tap a hoop, or swipe up from the ball. Tapping the ball throws to the best hoop.
- **Scores:** big low hoops are worth 10 and 20. Small high hoops are worth 30, 50 and 100.
- **Rounds:** 15 baskets per round. Misses are free: the ball bounces back and the kid tries again.
- **No fail:** no lives, no timers, no game over. A hidden helper keeps about 3 in 4 shots going in, and there are never 3 misses in a row.
- **Stages (one new idea each):**
  1. Toss – three big hoops
  2. Up High – far hoops worth more
  3. Slide – hoops slide side to side
  4. Bounce – hoops bob up and down
  5. Gold – gold hoops score double
  6. Colors – the announcer calls a color for bonus points
  7. Steps – sink 10 → 20 → 30 → 50 → 100 in order for a big bonus (plan ahead)
  8. Party – everything moves
  9. Play All – unlocks after stage 8, mixes it up
- **Prizes:** every round earns tickets. Spend them on 12 prizes (bear, duck, frog and more). Prizes sit on the booth shelf forever.
- **Help:** a hand shows the first throw. After 5 seconds idle the best hoop glows; after 10 seconds the voice gives a nudge. The **?** button always gives the next hint.
- **Adapts quietly:** moving hoops speed up after quick wins and slow down after misses.

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the game link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it once while online. After that it works offline.

## Grown-ups panel

Press and hold the **gear** (title or map screen) for 2 seconds.

- Sound, music, voice on/off
- Help level: Low, Med, High
- Kid's name (the announcer uses it)
- Last-round reminder after 3 or 5 rounds

No ads, purchases, links, accounts or data collection. Progress is saved on the device only.

## Tips

- Turn on **Guided Access** (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) and triple-click the side button to keep your kid in the game.
- If there's no sound, check the iPhone silent switch.
