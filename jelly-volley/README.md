# Jelly Volley

Two jelly domes, one beach ball, one net. Slide left and right, jump, and let the curve of your jelly send the ball over. Where the ball hits the dome decides where it goes: the top sends it up, the side sends it forward. For kids 5-9 (built for 5, with depth that shows up as they get better).

## How it plays

- **Move** with the left and right buttons. **Jump** with the big up button.
- A glowing ring on the ground shows where to stand to catch the ball.
- First to 5 points wins the round (grown-ups can pick 3, 5 or 7).
- No lives, no game over. A missed ball just means the next one comes in under two seconds.

### Six places, each with one new idea

| Place | New idea | Opponent |
|---|---|---|
| Beach | Basics | Pip |
| Park | The opponent jumps | Moss |
| Windy | Wind pushes the ball (watch the arrow) | Breeze |
| Snow | A taller net, so jump for it | Frost |
| Moon | Low gravity, floaty ball | Luna |
| Lava | All of it together | King Blob |

Winning a round opens the next place.

### Things to collect (they never reset)

- **Stars** for every return and every point. Stars open 7 hats.
- **Prizes** (stickers) for winning each place, a first point, a spike, a gold ball, a big rally, and 5 and 20 wins.
- **Six jelly colors** to pick from.
- Surprises: a rare **gold ball** worth 2 points, and **SPIKE!** when you jump and smash it down.

## Help built in

- **First run:** a hand shows Move, then Jump, then Hit the ball. Skip button if a grown-up wants to jump ahead.
- **Stuck for 5 seconds:** the right arrow button glows.
- **Stuck for 10 seconds:** a spoken nudge ("Go to the ring!").
- **Two misses in a row:** a helper hand steps in and moves the jelly under the ball once. The kid never misses 3 in a row.
- **? button:** first tap shows the ring and says where to go; second tap brings the helper.
- **Idle 18 seconds:** the game pauses itself with a big Play button.
- Difficulty adapts quietly: two wins in a row makes the opponent a bit sharper, two losses makes it a bit softer.

## Install on iPad or iPhone

1. Open the game link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it once from the home screen while online. After that it works offline.

Best played sideways. On a phone held upright it still works but shows a "Turn me!" hint.

## Grown-ups panel

On the title screen, **press and hold the gear for 2 seconds**.

- Sound effects, Music, Voice on/off
- Help level: Lots (default), Some, Little
- Points to win: 3, 5, 7
- Controls: Buttons, or Finger (drag anywhere to move, tap to jump)
- Kid's name (the announcer uses it: "Max wins!")
- Last round, then rest: after the next round the game shows a calm "All done!" screen, then turns this off by itself
- Reset progress (hold 3 seconds)

## Guided Access tip

Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access, turn it on. Open the game, triple-click the side (or home) button, tap Start. Your kid stays in the game until you triple-click again and enter your passcode.

## Good to know

- No ads, no purchases, no links out, no accounts, no data collected. Progress is saved only on the device.
- If the iPhone silent switch is on, sound effects and music may be muted. Voice usually still plays.
- Everything (art, music, sounds) is made in code, so there is nothing to download after the first visit.
