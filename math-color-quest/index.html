Grown-ups

Numbers mode: classic color by number. Math mode: each paint shows a problem; the answer is the number on its spots. Pinch or use the magnifier to zoom. Math journey: 1-2 numbers and counting, 3-4 adding, 5-6 taking away, 7 mixed, 8 doubles, 9 adding to 20, 10 taking away from 20, 11 missing numbers, 12 mixed to 20. Help adjusts quietly as your child plays.

Tip: iPhone/iPad Guided Access (Settings > Accessibility) keeps your child in the game. No ads, no purchases, no data leaves this device.
